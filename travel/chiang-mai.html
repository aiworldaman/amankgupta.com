Chiang Mai / temples, mountains & family time.

A family trip into the hills of Northern Thailand — misty peaks and royal pagodas on Doi Inthanon, roaring waterfalls, green rice fields, and a long day out to the extraordinary White Temple of Chiang Rai.

3 chapters 19 photos Chiang Mai · Chiang Rai Family trip
Chapter 01Chiang Mai city

Golden temples in the old city.

Chiang Mai is a city of temples — hundreds of them, tucked between cafés and night markets. Carved gold façades by day, glowing lanterns by night.

Old CityTemplesNight walks
Chapter 02Doi Inthanon

Up to the roof of Thailand.

A day in the mountains: the twin royal pagodas wrapped in flower gardens, cool air at the summit, waterfalls thundering through the jungle and rice fields in every shade of green on the way down.

Royal pagodasWaterfallsRice fields
Chapter 03Chiang Rai day trip

White, blue and gold.

A long, memorable day north: the dazzling White Temple — every surface carved and mirrored — the deep-blue Blue Temple, and the Golden Triangle, where Thailand, Laos and Myanmar meet on the Mekong.

White TempleBlue TempleGolden Triangle

Every trip brings back a fresh perspective — this one brought back a camera roll full of temples and memories our family will keep.
