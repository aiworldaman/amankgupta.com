Phu Quoc / beaches, bright lights & family time.

An island getaway in southern Vietnam: lazy beach days at the resort, glowing canals and a gondola ride in Grand World, the famous Kiss Bridge over the sea and a day of castles at VinWonders.

3 chapters 20 photos Phu Quoc · Vietnam Family trip
Chapter 01Resort & beach

Sea views, sand and slow mornings.

Days by the water: a balcony looking over palms and pools, long walks on soft sand, waves for the little ones and nowhere to be.

BeachResortPool
Chapter 02Grand World

Venice, Vietnamese style.

After dark, Grand World lights up: candy-coloured buildings, canals glowing green and gold, a gondola ride with the whole family and light tunnels to wander through.

Chapter 03Sunset Town & VinWonders

Bridges that almost kiss.

In the south of the island, the famous Kiss Bridge reaches out over the sea and stops just short of touching, then a day of castles and rides at VinWonders.

Kiss BridgeSunset TownVinWonders

Sun by day, lights by night — Phu Quoc gave us a week of beaches and bright canals and memories our family will keep.
